## 作品說明

本作為私人客製化創作，源於委託人受遊戲《沉默之丘 f》女主角啟發，而希望以此形象與我展開創作。油桐花與彼岸花，一白一紅，在盛放與凋零之間彼此映照。裂痕逐漸蔓延，像是破碎，也像蛻變的開始。她究竟正在走向終結，還是另一種新生？或許生與死之間，本就沒有一條清晰的界線。

<div class="artwork-description-en" lang="en" markdown="1">

## About the Artwork

This privately commissioned work was inspired by the female protagonist of the game *Silent Hill f*. Tung blossoms and spider lilies, one white and one red, reflect each other in their blooming and withering. Cracks gradually spread, like shattering, or perhaps the beginning of transformation. Is she heading towards her end, or another kind of rebirth? Perhaps there is no clear line between life and death.
